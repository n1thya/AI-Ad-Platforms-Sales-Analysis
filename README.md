# AI Ad Platforms: Quarterly Sales Analysis

An Excel workbook comparing four companies in the app advertising space: AppLovin, Reddit, Pinterest and Duolingo. It covers 8 quarters (Q3 2024 – Q2 2026), using figures from each company's earnings releases and SEC filings.


## What it shows
- Reddit grew revenue fastest in Q2 2026 (61% YoY), and its revenue outpaced user growth by 43 points, so monetization rather than audience drove the gain.
- AppLovin is the most profitable, with an 84% adjusted EBITDA margin.
- Duolingo's users grew faster than its revenue (23% vs 18%).
- Company guidance implies Q3 2026 growth of 47% (AppLovin), 48% (Reddit), 14% (Pinterest) and 11% (Duolingo).

## How it's built
- **Raw Data:** one Excel table of reported quarterly revenue, adjusted EBITDA, users and ARPU
- **Analysis:** SUMIFS grids for quarter-over-quarter and year-over-year growth, margin, indexed revenue, revenue per user, and guidance midpoints
- **Summary:** a scorecard driven by INDEX/MATCH (change the quarter cell and it recalculates), takeaway sentences generated by formulas, and six charts
- **Pinterest Regions:** revenue mix by geography, with a check that the regional figures add up to reported revenue
- **Sources:** links to every earnings release used

All figures are as reported. AppLovin's figures are continuing operations only, because it sold its apps business in 2025.
